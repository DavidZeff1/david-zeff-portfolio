About

I’m a Computer Science graduate (Sapir College) working as a Financial Analyst at the Gush Etzion Regional Council, where I review budgets in the tens of millions of shekels and build the reporting council departments use to make decisions. Alongside that I’ve taken four analytics projects end to end — churn, margin, campaign measurement and retail traffic — from raw extracts through to interactive dashboards.

What I care about is the part that usually gets skipped: checking the grain before trusting a metric, comparing like with like, and saying plainly what a result can’t prove. I completed data analysis and data science certificates at the Hebrew University and Bar-Ilan University in July 2026.
